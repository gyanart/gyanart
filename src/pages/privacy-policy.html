Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains how GyanArt handles information when you visit this website, request a growth audit, or contact us about our services.

1. Who we are

GyanArt is a founder-led digital growth consultancy operated by Gyaneshwar in India. For questions about this policy or your personal information, contact info@gyanart.com.

2. Information you provide

If you submit a contact form or request a growth audit, we may receive your name, business name, email address, mobile number, website or Google Maps link, and the message or goals you choose to share. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, or sensitive personal information through these forms.

3. How we use information

We use the information you provide to respond to enquiries, assess a requested audit, communicate about possible services, deliver agreed work, maintain business records, protect the website, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Website analytics

GyanArt may use Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, or related Google measurement tools to understand website visits and improve the website. Depending on the configuration, these tools may collect information such as pages visited, approximate location derived from network information, device and browser details, referral source, and interactions with pages. Cookies or similar technologies may be used. Google Tag Manager is a tag-management tool; the tags configured through it determine what data is collected. This policy does not describe advertising or cross-site ad targeting as a purpose.

Analytics use depends on which tools are actually enabled and how they are configured. Google may process information under its own terms and privacy policy. Learn more at Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Analytics’ data safeguards information.

5. Sharing and service providers

We may use service providers that help operate the website, receive form submissions, send email, or provide analytics. They may process information only as needed to provide their services or as otherwise permitted by law. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, security, and the website.

6. Retention and security

We keep enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage a potential or active client relationship, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, and meet legal requirements. Retention periods may vary by record type. We use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to provide optional information, although that may limit our ability to assess a request. You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping requirements. You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings; blocking them may affect some website functionality or measurement.

8. Children

This website and our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

9. External websites

This website may link to services such as LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, Google, or WhatsApp. Those services operate under their own privacy policies, and GyanArt does not control their practices.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email info@gyanart.com.
