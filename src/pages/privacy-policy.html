Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains how GyanArt handles information when you visit this website, request a growth audit, or contact us about services.

GyanArt is a digital growth consultancy operated by Gyaneshwar as an individual / sole proprietor in Bengaluru, India. For questions about this policy or your personal information, contact info@gyanart.com.

2. Information you provide

If you submit a contact form or request a growth audit, we may receive your name, business name, email address, mobile number, website or Google Maps link, and the message or goals you choose to share. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, or sensitive personal information through these forms.

3. How we use information

We use the information you provide to respond to enquiries, assess a requested audit or Starter Website offer enquiry, communicate about possible services, deliver agreed work, maintain business records, protect the website, and meet applicable legal obligations. On the Starter Website offer form, you may separately opt in to occasional GyanArt offers and website tips. This checkbox is optional and is not selected by default. We record the choice with your enquiry. You can withdraw marketing consent at any time by emailing info@gyanart.com. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Website analytics and cookies

This website includes a Google Analytics 4 (GA4) measurement tag configured with measurement ID G-D0JCS746B6. The tag is intended to measure website usage, which may include pages viewed, browser and device information, referral source, approximate location derived from network information, and interactions with the site. Cookies or similar technologies may be used depending on the visitor’s browser and Google Analytics configuration. GyanArt has added event tracking for successful contact and free growth-audit form submissions (event: generate_lead) and clicks on WhatsApp, telephone, and email links (event: contact_click). These events record the form category or contact method, not the information typed into the forms. Form fields and message contents are not intentionally sent to Google Analytics. The tag being present in the website build does not by itself confirm that events are successfully received by Google Analytics; configuration and data collection should be checked periodically. Analytics is used for website measurement, not as a statement of advertising or cross-site ad targeting.

Google may process analytics information under its own terms and privacy policy. Read Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Analytics’ data safeguards information. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings.

5. Form submissions, email and service providers

Website forms are handled by a PHP script on the web hosting service, which attempts to send messages using the host’s mail function. The hosting provider and email systems involved may process form details as part of delivering and handling those messages. Successful email delivery is not guaranteed. We may also use providers to host the website or provide analytics. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect rights, security, and the website.

Information should be kept only for as long as reasonably needed to respond to the enquiry, manage potential or active client work, maintain necessary business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal requirements. A specific retention schedule has not been established and should be reviewed. Reasonable measures are intended to protect information, but no website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.
