Redesign or improve?

Choose the smallest change that solves the real problem.

A full redesign is not automatically the best option. The recommendation should follow a review of the existing website and the problems customers are experiencing.

Improve existing pages

Best when the structure is sound but content, hierarchy, visual clarity or calls to action need attention.

Redesign selected sections

Useful when a few important service or landing pages are confusing, outdated or difficult to use.

Rebuild the website

Consider when the platform, architecture, accessibility or performance constraints prevent meaningful improvements.

Plan a safe migration

Inventory existing URLs, preserve valuable pages, map changed URLs and test redirects before launch.
