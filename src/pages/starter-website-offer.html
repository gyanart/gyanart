07 / A few fair questions

Good to know
before we begin.

If something isn't covered here, ask us directly. We'll give you a clear answer before you decide.

Is ₹4,999 really the one-time price?+

Yes. ₹4,999 covers the agreed Starter Website setup. There is no mandatory monthly subscription in this offer. Domain, hosting and any other agreed third-party costs are separate and will be explained before you commit.

How many pages do I get?+

The page structure depends on the business and will be agreed with you before the project starts. This is a small-business Starter Website offer, not an unlimited-pages or custom-application package.

What do I need to provide?+

Usually your business details, services, contact information and any photos or branding you already have. We'll explain what's needed for your project.

Will my website appear on Google?+

We'll include basic on-page search setup. Google decides how and when pages are indexed or ranked, so no specific search position or number of enquiries is guaranteed.

How long will it take?+

That depends on the agreed scope and when content is ready. We'll confirm an estimated delivery schedule before work starts.

Can I add more features later?+

Yes. Additional pages, ecommerce, booking features, maintenance and other services can be discussed and quoted separately whenever you need them.
