What the fees include

One-time setup fees cover a standard small-business website with responsive design and development within the package page limit. The client supplies or agrees the content and provides timely feedback and required access.

Not included unless agreed in writing: e-commerce, booking systems, membership areas, custom applications, complex integrations, large-scale copywriting, paid themes/plugins, domain registration, hosting, advertising management, advertising spend and other third-party costs.

Monthly support is scoped, not unlimited. It covers the maintenance, reviews and small content/page updates listed in the selected package. Larger redesigns, new functionality, extra pages and additional development are quoted separately.

Page limits refer to standard content pages. Special functionality or unusually complex pages may require a separate estimate. Final scope, deliverables, timeline, revisions, payment schedule and any exclusions are confirmed in a written proposal before work begins. No specific search rankings, leads or revenue outcomes are guaranteed.
