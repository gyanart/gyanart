How monthly plans work

Clear scope before work starts.

Before a plan begins, we’ll agree in writing on the priority activities, specific deliverables and quantities where applicable, reporting frequency, client responsibilities, exclusions, payment schedule and contract term.

What to expect

  • Reporting: a monthly activity/performance summary, with metrics dependent on access to reliable analytics and platform data.
  • Setup: any onboarding, tracking, account cleanup or one-time implementation fee is quoted separately if required.
  • Exclusions: ad spend, paid tools, hosting, domain fees, premium assets and third-party charges are excluded unless explicitly included in writing.
  • Scope control: new pages, large content batches, development, integrations or work outside the agreed plan require separate approval and may be quoted separately.
  • Results: rankings, traffic, enquiries and sales are not guaranteed.

Advertising spend is not included in the monthly fee and is paid directly by the client to the advertising platform. Monthly plan fees are service fees; they do not automatically include a new website or a full rebuild.
