How much does a website design cost in Bangalore? (2026 Price Guide)

Whether you are launching a new startup in HSR Layout, running a healthcare clinic in Whitefield, or managing a restaurant in Koramangala, website design costs in Bangalore vary dramatically. Here is a realistic, transparent breakdown of prices across freelancers, studios, and agencies in 2026.

1. Realistic Bangalore website pricing tiers in 2026

When searching for web designers in Bangalore, quotes can range anywhere from ₹5,000 to ₹5,00,000+. For small businesses, professional consultants, and local service providers, realistic market tiers break down as follows:

Basic Business Presence (₹10,000 – ₹20,000)

Best for single-location shops, individual consultants, dentists, and local trades who need a clean, professional digital brochure.

  • Typical scope: 1 to 5 responsive pages (Home, About, Services, Contact, FAQs).
  • Key features: Fast loading speed, mobile-first responsiveness, click-to-call, WhatsApp chat integration, and Google Maps embed.
  • Timeline: 1 to 2 weeks.
  • GyanArt equivalent: Digital Foundation Package (₹10,000 setup).

Enquiry & Growth Builder (₹25,000 – ₹45,000)

Ideal for established clinics, consulting firms, architecture studios, and growing local businesses that need tailored page structures and search visibility.

  • Typical scope: Up to 10–15 structured pages, dedicated service landing pages, and content strategy.
  • Key features: Custom UX styling, conversion-optimized contact funnels, Local SEO foundation, structured schema markup, and GA4 event tracking.
  • Timeline: 2 to 4 weeks.
  • GyanArt equivalent: Enquiry Builder Package (₹25,000 setup).

Comprehensive Growth & Custom Platform (₹37,000 – ₹80,000+)

Designed for businesses needing larger sites, custom booking systems, multi-location architecture, or online transactions.

  • Typical scope: Up to 30 pages, custom CMS setup (WordPress/Shopify/Headless), or multi-category catalogues.
  • Key features: Payment gateway integration (Razorpay/UPI), advanced event funnels, deep performance tuning, and multilingual architecture.
  • Timeline: 4 to 8 weeks.
  • GyanArt equivalent: Growth Optimization Package (₹37,000 setup).

2. Freelancer vs. Agency vs. Founder-Led Studio

Who you hire in Bangalore significantly impacts not just your upfront cost, but communication quality, turnaround speed, and long-term results.

Bangalore Freelancers (₹10,000 – ₹40,000)

Freelancers offer low overhead and direct communication. However, quality varies widely across Bangalore's gig market. Many freelance developers rely on pre-made WordPress templates with bloated plugins that slow down mobile performance. Post-launch support can also be inconsistent if the freelancer moves to a full-time job.

Traditional Digital Agencies (₹60,000 – ₹2,50,000+)

Established agencies in Indiranagar, Koramangala, or MG Road provide multidisciplinary teams. However, substantial portions of your fee go toward account managers, office overhead, and sales commissions. Furthermore, small-business accounts are frequently handed off to junior interns rather than senior strategists.

Founder-Led Design Studio (GyanArt Model)

A founder-led UX studio bridges this gap: you work directly with a senior UX consultant with 20+ years of digital experience at transparent, freelancer-level rates (₹10,000 to ₹37,000 setup), without paying for agency bureaucracy or fluff.

3. Hidden and ongoing costs to budget for

Beyond the one-time website design and development fee, every live website incurs operational expenses:

  • Domain name: ₹800 to ₹1,200 per year for a standard .com or .in registration via Cloudflare, Namecheap, or GoDaddy.
  • Web hosting: ₹2,500 to ₹6,000 per year for reliable shared or cloud hosting (e.g., Hostinger, Cloudways, or AWS) with SSL certification.
  • Ongoing maintenance & updates: ₹1,000 to ₹3,000 per month for software updates, security monitoring, regular backups, and minor content adjustments.
  • Content & copywriting: If you cannot supply your own text, professional copywriting usually costs ₹2,000 to ₹5,000 per page.

Frequently asked questions about website design costs

Why do some agencies quote ₹5,000 while others quote ₹1,00,000?

A ₹5,000 website is almost always an automated, cookie-cutter template where the agency swaps in your logo and leaves generic placeholder text. A custom ₹30,000+ website involves customer journey mapping, mobile responsiveness, fast custom code, Local SEO optimization, and conversion tracking designed to generate actual enquiries.

Will a new website automatically rank #1 on Google?

No honest professional can guarantee #1 rankings. A search-ready website provides the technical foundation, clean speed, and structured content necessary to compete, but ranking prominence requires ongoing content relevance, reviews, and authority.
