Website UX / Lead generation

Why is your website not generating leads?

A website can receive visits and still produce few enquiries. The problem may be a mismatch between the visitor’s intent and the page, unclear service information, weak trust signals or a difficult next step. Start by finding where the customer journey breaks—not by redesigning everything immediately.

Start with diagnosis

Six common reasons visitors do not enquire.

1. The page does not match the search

A visitor searching for a specific service lands on a generic homepage and cannot quickly confirm that you solve their problem. Give important services clear, relevant pages.

2. Your offer is hard to understand

Vague headlines and internal jargon make visitors work too hard. Explain who you help, what you do, where you operate and what happens next.

3. Important information is missing

Visitors may need service details, pricing guidance, location, process, examples or answers to common concerns before contacting you.

4. Trust is not established

Use genuine project examples, clear business information, authentic reviews where available and transparent scope. Never invent testimonials or results.

5. Contact actions are difficult

Make the preferred next step visible. Check that phone, WhatsApp, forms, booking links and email work on mobile.

6. You are not measuring enquiries

Without working event tracking and form testing, you cannot tell whether visitors are taking action or where they abandon the journey.

Test the contact path on a phone and confirm that every enquiry is received and measured.

Use evidence, not guesses

What to inspect in your analytics.

  • Search Console: queries, landing pages, impressions, clicks and click-through rate.
  • Analytics: landing-page engagement and the sequence of pages visited before an enquiry.
  • Conversion events: successful form submissions, phone clicks and WhatsApp clicks. Test events rather than assuming they work.
  • Lead records: enquiry source, business fit, response time and whether the enquiry became a client.

Do not treat low conversion as a design problem until you have checked traffic quality, offer relevance, tracking and whether the contact channel is functioning.
