The Diagnostic Approach

Four trust barriers that stop high-value clients from reaching out

In professional services, transactions are high-consideration. When prospective corporate clients hesitate, friction usually stems from one of four pitfalls:

BARRIER 01

Vague, all-encompassing service lists

Claiming to do everything from tax audits to brand strategy dilutes credibility. Specialization inspires confidence; generalization creates doubt.

BARRIER 02

Hidden starting fees and opaque scopes

Corporate clients and business owners dread open-ended hourly billing without clear milestones. Transparent baseline pricing reduces anxiety.

BARRIER 03

Faceless corporate anonymity

Clients hire experienced individuals, not faceless entities. Featuring the founder's background, philosophy, and authentic photos builds rapport before the first call.

BARRIER 04

Exhausting enquiry intake forms

Asking for annual turnover, company registration numbers, and 10 required fields before a first conversation drives away qualified prospects.
