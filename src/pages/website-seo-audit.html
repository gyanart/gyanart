What the review covers

A practical review across the parts of SEO that work together.

The exact depth depends on your website size, goals and available access. The review focuses on issues that affect discoverability, page usefulness and the customer journey.

Crawlability and indexability

Check accessible pages, index directives, canonical signals, redirects, sitemap references and obvious crawl barriers.

On-page SEO and search intent

Review page titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, page focus and alignment with the searches your customers use.

Content and page quality

Identify unclear, thin, duplicated or incomplete information and opportunities to answer customer questions more usefully.

Internal links and site structure

Look for important pages that are difficult to reach, broken internal links, weak contextual links and overlapping page topics.

Mobile experience and performance

Review mobile usability and visible performance concerns. Where suitable data is available, consider relevant Core Web Vitals signals.

Local search signals

For businesses serving a defined area, review relevant location information and the consistency of website and Google Business Profile details.
