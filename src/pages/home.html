The customer journey

Why do local business websites fail to convert visitors into enquiries?

Most local websites fail because they describe what the business does instead of helping customers take action. A high-converting local website answers three vital questions within 5 seconds: what specific service you provide, whether you serve their Bangalore neighborhood, and how easily they can call or WhatsApp you.
