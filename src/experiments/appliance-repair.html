Growth experiment / concept

Appliance repair: help customers reach you quickly.

This sample idea is for a local repair business. When a fridge or washing machine breaks, people want to know if you cover their area, when you can come and how to contact you.

Customer problem

Customers want a nearby technician they can trust and reach without delay.

Journey

Search → Maps → Reviews → Availability → WhatsApp or call → Service.

Potential improvements

Keep your Google listing current, list the appliances you repair, explain your service area and warranty, and make calling or WhatsApp easy.

How to measure

Track calls, WhatsApp clicks, service-area enquiries and completed lead outcomes where available.

Concept only. No business or performance results are represented.
