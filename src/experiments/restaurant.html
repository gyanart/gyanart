Restaurant: help nearby diners decide to visit.

This sample idea shows how a restaurant can help people check the menu, opening hours, location and reviews before deciding to visit. It is not a real client project.

Customer problem

People want to see your menu, photos, opening hours, location and reviews before choosing where to eat.

Journey

Discover → Evaluate → Desire → Visit.

Potential improvements

Keep your Google listing accurate, share real photos, make the menu easy to find and provide clear directions or booking details.

How to measure

Track direction requests, calls, reservation actions, menu views and campaign outcomes where available.

Concept only. No restaurant client or results are implied.
