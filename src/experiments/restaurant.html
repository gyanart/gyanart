A restaurant page that helps nearby diners decide to visit.

Menu and food

Make the current menu easy to read on a phone. Clearly label vegetarian options, allergens and prices where accurate, and update items when they change.

Atmosphere and occasion

Use authentic photos and explain the dining experience, suitable occasions and available seating without misleading visitors.

Plan a visit

Keep opening hours, address, directions, contact details and reservation instructions accurate and consistent with the Google Business Profile.

Clear next action

Help visitors choose whether to view the menu, call, make a reservation or get directions. Only show booking options the restaurant actually supports.

Suggested customer journey

Turn local discovery into a confident visit

1. Discover

Provide accurate location, cuisine/category, opening hours and local search information.

2. Evaluate

Help people review the menu, prices, authentic photos, dietary information and visit details.

3. Plan

Make directions, calling and reservation steps clear, especially on mobile.

4. Learn

Measure menu views, calls, direction requests and reservation actions where tracking is available.
