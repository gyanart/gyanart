Growth experiment / concept

Restaurant: turn discovery into a visit.

A fictional concept for a local restaurant that wants potential guests to move from discovery to a confident decision to visit.

Customer problem

People compare cuisine, menu, ambience, photos, reviews, location and opening hours before deciding where to eat.

Journey

Discover → Evaluate → Desire → Visit.

Potential improvements

Accurate Maps information, compelling real photos, easy-to-find menu, current hours, review responses and clear directions/reservation options.

How to measure

Track direction requests, calls, reservation actions, menu views and campaign outcomes where available.

Concept only. No restaurant client or results are implied.
