Growth experiment / concept

Dental clinic: from local search to appointment.

This fictional concept explores how a dental clinic could make it easier for patients to compare care options and book confidently. It is not a client case study.

Customer problem

Patients compare nearby clinics, treatments, doctor experience, reviews, fees and appointment availability.

Journey

Search → Maps → Reviews and expertise → Treatment information → Appointment.

Potential improvements

Clear treatment pages, practitioner credentials, transparent next steps, location details and visible booking/contact actions.

How to measure

Track calls, appointment enquiries, booking clicks, direction requests and relevant landing-page engagement.

Concept only. No clinic is represented and no performance results are claimed.
