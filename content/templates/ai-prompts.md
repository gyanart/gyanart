# Reusable AI prompts for GyanArt

Use one prompt per stage. Replace bracketed fields. Provide source notes and actual GyanArt page URLs where possible.

## 1. Topic research and prioritisation

You are the editorial researcher for GyanArt, a founder-led digital growth consultancy serving small businesses in Bengaluru, India.

Audience: owners/managers of local service businesses. Initial focus: appliance repair and other home-service providers; later dentists, restaurants and professional services.

Topic/question: [CUSTOMER QUESTION]
Business goal: attract relevant enquiries for [SERVICE]
Known customer observations: [OBSERVATIONS OR "NONE YET"]
Research notes and source URLs: [PASTE VERIFIED NOTES]

Produce:
1. The likely search intent and what the reader needs to decide or do.
2. The exact question the article should answer in the first 100 words.
3. Five subquestions the article should address.
4. What the existing search results appear to cover and a useful gap to fill, based only on results supplied.
5. A unique angle grounded in the supplied observations.
6. Primary keyword and natural related phrases, without search-volume claims unless supplied with a source.
7. Claims that require additional verification.
8. A recommendation: write now, research more, or reject as too broad/competitive/irrelevant.

Do not invent search data, competitor findings, statistics, quotes, customer feedback or sources. Mark unknowns explicitly.

## 2. Source-led research brief

Using the question [QUESTION], prepare a brief using only these supplied sources:
[URL + title + date checked + relevant excerpt/notes]

For every factual claim, map it to the source that supports it. Prefer official documentation, primary research, government sources and first-party platform guidance for platform-specific claims. Separate facts, interpretations and recommendations. Flag contradictions and missing evidence. Do not cite a source you have not been given or inspected.

## 3. Article draft

Write a practical, original article for GyanArt from the approved brief below.

Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Reader question: [QUESTION]
Search intent: [INTENT]
Primary keyword: [KEYWORD]
Useful angle: [ANGLE]
Relevant GyanArt service page: [URL]
Verified research notes and source URLs: [NOTES]
Original experience/examples available: [EXAMPLES OR "NONE"]

Requirements:
- Answer the main question directly near the beginning.
- Use a clear H1, useful H2/H3 headings, concise paragraphs, and practical steps.
- Include a checklist, decision criteria, or worked example where it genuinely helps.
- Explain Indian/Bengaluru context only when relevant and supported.
- Use natural language; no keyword stuffing, fake personal anecdotes, fabricated case studies, or unsupported promises.
- Add contextual internal-link suggestions using only these real pages: [PASTE CURRENT SITE URLS].
- Cite external claims in the article with readable source links.
- Finish with a useful, low-pressure next step connected to the relevant GyanArt service.
- Do not claim guaranteed rankings, leads, revenue or AI-search visibility.
- If evidence is missing, insert [VERIFY: claim] rather than inventing an answer.

Return: title, slug, meta description, article body, suggested internal links, source list, and a separate list of claims needing human verification.

## 4. Factual and SEO audit

Audit the draft below as a sceptical editor, not as its author.

Check:
- factual claims and whether each has an adequate supplied source;
- unsupported numbers, dates, superlatives and guarantees;
- whether it answers the reader's actual question quickly;
- originality and whether it adds practical value beyond a generic summary;
- primary keyword fit without stuffing;
- title, H1, meta description, heading structure and slug;
- internal links (correct URL, relevant anchor text, useful destination);
- external sources (authority, relevance and whether the cited page supports the claim);
- readability, mobile scanning, accessibility and CTA relevance;
- potentially misleading statements or claims that imply results GyanArt has not demonstrated.

Return a table: issue, severity (blocker/high/medium/low), exact location, reason, recommended correction. Do not rewrite the entire article unless asked. Never mark a factual claim verified merely because it sounds plausible.

## 5. Final pre-publication review

Given the latest article and checklist results, return one status:
- BLOCKED: factual, legal, privacy, attribution or critical-link issue remains;
- REVISE: useful article, but material edits are needed;
- READY FOR HUMAN APPROVAL: checks look good, but the named owner must still approve.

List unresolved issues and all URLs that need a final manual click test. You cannot grant approval or publish on behalf of Gyaneshwar.
